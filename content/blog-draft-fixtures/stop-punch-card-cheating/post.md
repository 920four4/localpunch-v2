Most punch card cheating comes from three gaps: a stamp or hole punch anyone can buy, a QR code that still works when someone photographs it, and no limit on how often one card can be punched. Close those three and you stop nearly all of it. Keep everything else simple, so honest regulars never feel like suspects.

## How do people actually cheat punch cards?

Openly, it turns out. A post on r/UnethicalLifeProTips suggests finding a coffee shop's loyalty stamp cheaply online for "infinite free coffee" ([Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/UnethicalLifeProTips/comments/f48nu7/ulpt_have_a_favourite_coffee_place_with_stamp/)). Another person confessed to buying a hole punch that matched a local coffee stand's and punching their own cards ([Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/confessions/comments/p5r90e/i_once_bought_a_hole_punch_that_matched_the_one_a/)).

The common ways it happens:

- **Copied stamps or punches.** Standard shapes are easy to match.
- **Screenshot scans.** A printed QR code that never changes can be photographed and scanned from home.
- **Double-dipping.** Two punches for one visit, or a friend at the counter adding extras.
- **Card swapping.** One person collects on several cards.

Most of your customers will never do any of this. The goal is to make cheating not worth the effort, not to question everyone.

## Fix 1: Make the punch impossible to copy

If you're on paper, use a custom stamp or an unusual punch shape, and add your initials or the date in pen when the card is full. It's not perfect, but it raises the effort.

If you're on a digital card, the punch should come from something only your counter has. A QR code that changes every few minutes, and that can only be used once, means a photo from last week is useless.

## Fix 2: Limit how often one card can be punched

Decide what a "visit" means for your shop. A coffee shop might allow one punch every few hours. A barber might allow one a week. Write the rule down so staff can say it without arguing: "One punch per visit, and visits are at least 12 hours apart."

This one limit stops most double-dipping quietly. Nobody has to accuse anyone.

## Fix 3: Keep the owner in charge of exceptions

Rules need a human override. A regular buying for the whole office deserves two punches. A customer who forgot their phone should still get credit. Let staff handle the normal case and leave exceptions to you or a shift lead.

And if a punch goes on the wrong card or gets added twice by mistake, fix it right away instead of letting it slide.

## What not to do

- Don't put up "cheaters will be banned" signs. Honest regulars read them too.
- Don't make the reward so big that cheating is worth it.
- Don't make everyone show ID to get a punch.
- Don't change the rules in the middle of someone's card.

## Checklist: a cheat-resistant punch card

- [ ] Paper: custom stamp or unusual punch shape
- [ ] Digital: QR code that changes and can be used only once
- [ ] A clear rule for what counts as a visit, with a minimum gap
- [ ] One sentence staff can say when someone asks for extra punches
- [ ] Only the owner or shift lead approves exceptions

- [ ] A reward that's generous but not worth faking

## FAQ

### Is punch card fraud actually common?

There's no good public data on how often it happens in small shops, so be wary of anyone quoting a number. People do openly share ways to fake stamps online, which is reason enough to close the easy gaps.

### Should I confront someone I think is cheating?

Keep it calm and about the rule, not the person: "We can only add one punch per visit."

### Will these rules put off honest customers?

Not if they're invisible. A regular should never notice the time limit or the changing QR code. They just get their punch.
