Taquerias live on repeat lunch traffic. One new regular who comes twice a week is worth more than a hundred one-time tourists who saw you on TikTok.

Here's how to turn first-time visitors into weekly regulars — without a marketing agency or a complicated app.

## The 10th taco still works

The classic "buy 9, get the 10th free" isn't lazy — it's **clear**. Your line cook doesn't need training. Your customer doesn't need to read fine print.

Digital doesn't change the reward. It changes what happens to lost cards and fake stamps.

## Speed matters at the register

During lunch rush, loyalty has to be faster than explaining the program.

1. Customer scans QR while waiting in line (or at the counter)
2. Staff punches in one tap when they pay
3. Done

If it takes longer than stamping paper, staff will skip it. Pick a system built for one tap.

## Talk about it once, loudly

You don't need a sermon. Two moments:

- **At the register:** "We're on phone punch cards now — scan that poster, tenth taco's free."
- **On the bag:** Sticker with the same QR as the poster.

Repeat the same sentence for two weeks until customers say it back to you.

## Fix slow Tuesdays with the list

Paper cards don't give you phone numbers. Digital ones do (with SMS consent).

Slow Tuesday text example:

> "Double punch today only — show your card at checkout."

You're not discounting the whole world. You're activating people who already like you.

## Don't over-reward tourists

If you're near a tourist corridor, consider a separate "locals" program or a higher punch count for visitors. Your regulars subsidize your business; tourists are bonus revenue.

## First week checklist

- [ ] Pick reward (9+1 is fine)
- [ ] Print QR poster for counter and window
- [ ] Train cashiers: one sentence + one tap
- [ ] Post QR on Instagram story
- [ ] Text your list on the first slow day
