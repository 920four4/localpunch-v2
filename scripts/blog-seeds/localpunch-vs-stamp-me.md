People search "Stamp Me alternative" when they're comparing options — not because Stamp Me is bad, but because they're trying to find the right fit for *their* shop.

This is an honest comparison. We built LocalPunch; we're biased. We'll say when Stamp Me might be the better pick.

## What Stamp Me does well

Stamp Me is a mature platform. Multi-location support, marketing tools, and a big customer base. If you're a small chain or you want campaigns beyond punch cards, Stamp Me has depth.

## What LocalPunch optimizes for

We optimize for one thing: **single-location local shops that want a punch card, not a marketing department.**

| | LocalPunch | Stamp Me (typical) |
|---|---|---|
| Pricing | $60/mo flat, unlimited | Tiered + location fees |
| Customer app | No download — browser wallet | Customer app common |
| Setup | ~10 minutes | Often more configuration |
| Feature scope | Punch + redeem + list | Broader marketing suite |

## When LocalPunch is the better fit

- You're a coffee shop, taqueria, barber, or boba shop with one counter
- You want transparent pricing on the website, no sales call
- Your customers won't download another app
- You care about a contact list you can text on slow days

## When Stamp Me might win

- You run multiple locations and need centralized reporting
- You want automated campaigns, segments, and advanced CRM-style tools
- You already have Stamp Me workflows your staff knows

## The pricing math (simplified)

Stamp Me's value shows up when you use the extra features. LocalPunch's value shows up when you **don't** — when you just need unlimited punches, unlimited customers, and a flat bill.

If you're paying for features you never open, you're subsidizing software built for a different business size.

## Bottom line

There's no universal winner. There's a right tool for your complexity level.

If you want the simplest path from paper card to digital list, [try LocalPunch free](/login?role=business). If you need enterprise-style loyalty marketing, Stamp Me deserves a serious look.
